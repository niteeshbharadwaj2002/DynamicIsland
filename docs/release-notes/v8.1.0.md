## NotchIQ v8.1.0

Two new ways to use the idle island (a control center and a notification center), a calmer symbols-only pill, a clearer privacy indicator, and tidier settings and links.

### New

- **Control center: tap the idle pill** ([#12](https://github.com/niteeshbharadwaj2002/NotchIQ/issues/12)). With nothing on the island and app shortcuts on, a tap opens a panel with a search field, battery and Bluetooth badges, quick tiles (Wi-Fi, Flashlight, Do Not Disturb, Island Settings) and the app launcher with a Utilities page. A long press still opens it. On the lockscreen only Flashlight and Do Not Disturb are offered.
- **Notification center: swipe down on the idle pill** ([#13](https://github.com/niteeshbharadwaj2002/NotchIQ/issues/13)). Live notifications grouped by app, with open, dismiss, Clear all and inline reply. A locked device shows counts only. Needs "pill swipe actions" turned on.

### Changed

- **The pill shows symbols, not words** ([#10](https://github.com/niteeshbharadwaj2002/NotchIQ/issues/10)). No more scrolling text on the pill or the companion bubble. Calls show the phone icon and the call time, with a pulsing dot while ringing or connecting. Messages show the app icon with an unread dot, or a count for a grouped pill, at a fixed width. Flashlight, live activity and navigation pills use dots or numbers. The full text stays in the expanded card and in TalkBack.
- **Privacy indicator is a pulsing circle** ([#11](https://github.com/niteeshbharadwaj2002/NotchIQ/issues/11)): green for the camera, orange for the microphone, half green and half orange when both are in use. TalkBack names the sensor.
- **Layout screen summaries** ([#16](https://github.com/niteeshbharadwaj2002/NotchIQ/issues/16)). Every group has a one-line gray description under its title, and settings search matches it.
- **Links point to NotchIQ** ([#15](https://github.com/niteeshbharadwaj2002/NotchIQ/issues/15)). Star, Request a feature, Report a bug, About and the welcome dialog all open this project. The Telegram option is removed. Credit links to the original author are kept.

### Known limits

- The swipe-down notification center was checked in an emulator only; its reliability on real devices, and reply, Clear all and dismiss, are not yet confirmed.
- Some strings still say "Smart Island", and the new strings are English only in other languages.
- The landscape corner pill does not open the control or notification center, and the control center has no brightness or volume sliders.

### Install

This is a signed build. It installs over 8.0.0 only if 8.0.0 was signed with the same key. Android 8.0 or newer.
